BOUNDARY SENSOR

Purpose:

Detect weakening, crossing, relocation, erosion, bypass, and redefinition of boundaries.

The Boundary Sensor does not determine whether a boundary is valid.

The Boundary Sensor observes how boundaries behave under pressure.
