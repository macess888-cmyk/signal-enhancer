RELATIONSHIP SENSOR

Purpose:

Detect the formation, evolution, weakening, strengthening, transfer, and persistence of relationships surrounding an object, signal, system, process, person, or capability.

The Relationship Sensor does not determine whether a relationship is valid.

The Relationship Sensor observes how relationships change over time.
