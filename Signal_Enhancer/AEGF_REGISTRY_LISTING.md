# AEGF Registry Listing

HACR Hybrid Observatory is an independent observability and inspection framework focused on pressure relocation, formation conditions, relationship-field evolution, and governance inspection surfaces.

The Observatory does not grant authority, admissibility, continuation rights, or execution permission. Its purpose is to localize where constraints, dependencies, standing, legitimacy pressures, and governance-relevant transitions emerge, relocate, or become visible before consequence occurs.

The work primarily sits within the transitions between Evidence, Trust, and Authority.
