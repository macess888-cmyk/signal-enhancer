# Question Generator

Given an observation, ask:

1. What survives?
2. What fails?
3. What remains alterable?
4. What remains reconstructable?
5. What assumptions are inherited?
6. What distinction became visible?
7. What new unknown appeared?
8. What pressure test would destroy the observation?
9. What would falsify the candidate geometry?
10. What is still unknowable?
