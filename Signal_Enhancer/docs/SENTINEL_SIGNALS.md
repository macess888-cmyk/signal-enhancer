SENTINEL SIGNALS

Purpose:

A Sentinel Signal is a harmless dummy signal used to reveal hidden response surfaces.

It does not test the real signal.

It tests the ecosystem response.

Real Signal asks:
What is happening?

Sentinel Signal asks:
Who notices?
Who responds?
Who protects?
Who intervenes?
Who reconstructs?
Who treats the signal as authoritative?

Potential detections:

- hidden authority
- hidden dependency
- hidden standing
- hidden preservation priority
- hidden intervention path
- hidden control surface
- hidden reconstruction pressure

Boundary:

Sentinel Signal ≠ Deception for authority
Sentinel Signal ≠ Decision
Sentinel Signal ≠ Intervention

It is a non-authoritative probe.
