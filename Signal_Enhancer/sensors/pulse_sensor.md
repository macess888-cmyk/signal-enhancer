PULSE SENSOR

Purpose:
Detect rhythm, cadence, recurrence, acceleration, deceleration, interruption, and irregularity in signal behavior.

Detects:
- repeated signals
- sudden silence
- acceleration
- slowing cadence
- instability
- abnormal rhythm

Primary question:
Is the signal pattern changing over time?

Outputs:
- pulse_normal
- pulse_irregular
- pulse_accelerating
- pulse_decelerating
- pulse_interrupted
- pulse_unknown

Boundary:
Pulse detection is not interpretation.
Pulse Sensor does not decide significance.

UNKNOWN → HOLD
